Understanding the Risks

Below you’ll find a full explanation of the risks associated with the things you do and share online. Not all actions are equally risky — context matters, so where you post something can make a big difference. For example, sharing your location privately with friends is very different from posting it publicly on a livestream. That’s why we’ve broken things down into separate tables based on the type of platform you’re using.

Check out the links below to explore each category in more detail:

  • Activities done online
  • Information shared on social media platforms
  • Information shared on online shopping and services platforms
  • Information shared on school or government platforms
  • Information shared on gaming platforms

The more you understand, the more in control you’ll be of your data footprint. Stay safe out there!
